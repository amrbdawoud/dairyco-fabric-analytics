# 0. Findings and recommendations

All figures are recomputed from the governed gold layer (`scripts/findings.py` → `docs/findings_numbers.json`) and match the semantic-model measures named below. Unless stated otherwise, the period is Jan 2025 – Aug 2026, and year-on-year is Jan–Aug 2026 vs Jan–Aug 2025.

## Headline

Net revenue grew **+7.6%** (7.55M → 8.13M EGP) and volume **+10.6%**, but gross profit fell **−5.4%** (2.15M → 2.03M). Gross margin dropped from **28.4% to 25.0%** as a clean step in January 2026. The bridge has two parts:
- **Mix: −1.50 pp.** Milk, the lowest-margin category, grew +109% and rose from 23% to 46% of revenue.
- **Cost: −1.94 pp.** Five Milk SKUs were costed at 6% above their ERP standard.

Nothing else moved: margins in Yogurt, Juice and Cheese are unchanged. Growth is real volume, but it is growth in the least profitable products, costed on the wrong basis.

## Three issues management did not ask about

1. **The target file is unusable.** Targets total 459.5M against 19.2M actual (≈ 24×), with every target implying exactly 35 EGP per unit and a regional split (Cairo 2.3× Canal) that does not match sales. Attainment is ≈ 4% everywhere. Commercial performance management currently has no valid yardstick. *Action:* Finance restates targets; the index is used meanwhile.
2. **The CRM pipeline is not being managed.** 91.9M of "open" pipeline, 91% of which is past its expected close date. Activity volume has no relationship with account revenue (r = 0.10), and CRM covers only Modern Trade and Key Accounts (24% of revenue). *Action:* pipeline hygiene rules, CRM coverage for Traditional Trade and Wholesale, and activity targets tied to outcomes.
3. **Master data and payroll controls.** There are 8 duplicate customer records carrying 819 invoices, 185 invoices with free-text regions, and territory codes shared across regions. **28 employees were paid before their hire date (112 employee-months).** *Action:* a customer MDM owner, and an HR/Finance payroll control review.
